About Fikko

Last updated 17 October 2026

Fikko is a daily health tracker. You log water, meals, medications and supplements, activity, sleep and mood in one quick check-in, and Fikko shows how it all adds up over time. It works in your web browser on any phone or computer, with nothing to download. Fikko is a general wellness product and is not a medical device or medical advice.

Fikko is run by PipePiper, a sole proprietorship registered in the Republic of Korea (business registration number 471-45-01158; 104-106, 127 Irwon-ro, Gangnam-gu, Seoul 06361). Contact: hello@fikko.io. Our main website is www.fikko.io.

How it works

  • Create a free account at app.fikko.io with your email or Google. You must be at least 14 years old.
  • Check in each day. Log what you drank, ate, took and did, and how you slept and felt, by typing, speaking or taking a photo of a meal.
  • See your trends. Fikko turns your logs into a weekly summary, a dashboard, and an AI coach that answers questions about your own habits.
  • Upgrade when you want more. The Free plan is yours to keep. Premium and Max add higher limits and more features.

Plans and prices

Prices are in US dollars and include any sales tax, VAT or GST that applies where you live. Subscriptions renew each month or year until you cancel.

  • Free, $0: 3 custom habits, medication and supplement tracking, basic metrics, steps and activity from one device, 5 AI logs a week, email reminders, 7-day trends and PDF reports of what you’ve logged.
