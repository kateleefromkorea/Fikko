Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

The short version: Fikko is ad-free. We use your information to run the app for you, we never sell it, and you can download or delete everything at any time from your Profile. If Fikko is ever sold, that promise goes with your data.

Who we are

Fikko is a habit and wellness tracking app run by PipePiper, a sole proprietorship (개인사업자) owned by Seok Hwan Lee and registered in the Republic of Korea. PipePiper is responsible for your personal information in Fikko.

We offer Fikko in Australia, Singapore, South Korea and the United States, and we follow the privacy laws that apply to you there, including Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), Australia’s Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles, Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA), and US state privacy laws such as Washington’s My Health My Data Act and the California Consumer Privacy Act.

PipePiper (owner: Seok Hwan Lee) · Business registration number 471-45-01158 · 104-106, 127 Irwon-ro, Gangnam-gu, Seoul 06361, Republic of Korea

For any privacy question or request, contact our Chief Privacy Officer, Seok Hwan Lee, at hello@fikko.io.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider; we never see your password). If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address from Google.
  • Profile: name, date of birth, sex (used only for the calorie formula), height, weight, activity level, goals, target weight, diet, allergies and your tracking preferences.
  • What you log: water, meals and foods, activity minutes, sleep times and how rested you felt, mood, medications and supplements you add, and any custom habits.
  • What you share: Community posts and comments, recipes and recipe photos, saves, cheers and reports.
  • Points and badges earned from recipes you share.
  • My Fikko: the seed you pick, when it was planted, the pot and companion you chose, and the plants you have harvested, with the reward you picked and whether a real tree has been planted for it. Growth is worked out from your habit log; nothing extra is collected.
  • AI recipes: recipe ideas you choose to save from “Cook with what you have”. Only you can see them.
  • AI coach conversations: the messages you send the coach and its replies, until you clear the chat, and a count of messages sent each day to apply the daily limit. For every AI feature (coach, voice check-ins, photo logging, interaction check) we also keep a usage record of each request: the feature, its size in tokens and what it cost us, never its content. We use it to manage costs and spot misuse.
  • Connected devices: you can connect Fitbit or Pixel Watch (through Google), with more devices coming. When you connect a device or health app, we receive your daily summaries from that service, such as: sleep stages and duration, resting heart rate, HRV, breathing rate, body temperature compared with your baseline, steps, active minutes, active calories, blood oxygen and VO₂ max. We only receive what you approve on the provider’s permission screen, and we only read data; we never write to your account there.
  • Technical data: a sign-in token kept in your browser so you stay signed in, small settings such as dismissed notes, and standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) kept by our hosting providers for security.
  • Visitor statistics: anonymous counts of which pages of our website and app are viewed, the website a visitor came from, the type of device (phone, tablet or computer) and the country. We don’t use cookies or store anything on your device for this. To count unique visitors without identifying anyone, our server combines your IP address and browser type with a code that changes every day and keeps only the scrambled result, for no more than two days. The counts are kept for about 13 months and can’t be linked to you or your account.

Some of what you log, such as medications, mood and sleep, and the data from connected devices, is health information. Korean and Australian law, and some US state laws, treat it as sensitive. We treat it with extra care and only use it to show you your own habits and insights and to run the features you choose to use.

How we use it

  • To run your account and show your habits, dashboard, plan and insights.
  • To work out your calorie target from the details you give us.
  • To filter recipes using your diet and allergies.
  • To run the AI features you choose to use (the AI coach, voice check-ins, photo logging, recipe ideas and the medication check), and to check that AI replies stay within general wellness and habit coaching.
  • To show what you choose to share to other signed-in members.
  • To keep Fikko safe, for example rate limits and acting on reports.
  • To answer you when you contact us.
  • To send you emails about your account: sign-in and security emails, notices about your plan (such as your founding-member year ending), and reminders you choose to turn on. Each reminder email has a link to turn reminders off.
  • To understand how Fikko is used, so we can improve it. We look at totals across all members, such as how many finish setup, log a habit each week or use a feature. We don’t use a third-party analytics service or tracking cookies for this, and we don’t build profiles of individual members.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your data to target advertising. We don’t “sell” or “share” personal information as US state privacy laws define those terms, and we never sell health information.

Who can see what

  • Only you: your profile, everything you log, your saved recipes and your points.
  • Other signed-in members: your Community posts and comments and the recipes and photos you share, shown with your first name and last initial (for example “Kate L.”). People who aren’t signed in can’t see them.

Your consent

When you join, and whenever this policy changes in an important way, Fikko asks for your consent before collecting anything about you. Each consent is a separate choice:

  • Required: the Terms and this policy (and that you’re 14 or older); collecting and using your personal information; collecting and using your health information; and storing your information outside your country with the providers below. Fikko can’t work without these.
  • Optional: the AI features, which send some of your data to Anthropic in the United States. Everything else works without them.

The notice you see follows the country you’re in, which we detect from your connection, and you can change it. We keep a record of each choice, the date, and the version of this policy you agreed to. You can turn the AI features off or on at any time in Profile → Privacy. To withdraw a required consent, delete your account.

How we store and protect your fitness and health data

  • Where: your account and everything you log, including data from connected devices, is stored in a Supabase database in Singapore.
  • Encryption: data is encrypted in transit (TLS) between your device, our servers and our providers, and encrypted at rest in the database.
  • Access: every table is locked to its owner, so no other member can read your logs. Keys for connected devices can only be read by our server. We don’t look at individual members’ logs: our admin tools show only anonymous totals. We would only access your data to help when you ask us to, or where the law requires it.
  • Use: only to show you your own habits, trends, targets and insights, and, if you turn them on, for the AI features below. Never for advertising, never sold, and never used to train AI models.
  • Deletion: as described in “How long we keep it” below. Disconnecting a device lets you delete what it synced.

AI features and your prompts

Fikko’s AI coach, voice check-ins, photo logging, recipe ideas and the AI part of the medication check use Anthropic’s Claude model. They’re optional and only work if you’ve agreed to them.

  • What’s sent: only what each feature needs, as listed under Service providers below. Never your email address, and never data from Google (Fitbit or Pixel Watch).
  • What the AI does: it writes suggestions about everyday habits and turns what you say or photograph into log entries for you to confirm. Its replies are automatic and nobody at Fikko reviews them. It doesn’t diagnose, give medical advice, or make decisions about you.
  • How long it’s kept: your coach conversation is stored in Fikko (in Singapore) until you clear the chat or delete your account. Meal photos aren’t stored by Fikko. Anthropic deletes what we send within 30 days, except that content its automated safety systems flag may be kept for up to 2 years. Anthropic doesn’t use it to train its models.
  • Your choice: switch AI features off in Profile → Privacy at any time. After that, nothing more is sent to Anthropic.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run Fikko. They process data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase for our database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • Anthropic to power Fikko’s AI features. When you message the AI coach, we send Anthropic’s Claude API your message, your recent conversation, your profile details (first name, age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goals and targets, diet and allergies), the number of medications you track (not their names), your last four weeks of habit logs (water, meals and calories, activity, sleep, mood, whether you ticked off your medications, and custom habits) and the foods you logged in the last seven days, so it can reply. To keep replies within general wellness, your message is also checked by Anthropic’s models before it’s answered, and each reply is checked before you see it; these checks see only your message and the coach’s replies. We don’t send your email address, date of birth, medication names, or data from Google (Fitbit or Pixel Watch). Voice check-ins also use Anthropic: we send what you said (as text) along with the names of the medications and custom habits you track, so it can tell which ones you mean; your habit history isn’t sent. Photo logging does too: when you log a meal from a photo, the picture is shrunk on your device and sent to Anthropic so it can identify the food and estimate portions. We don’t keep the photo (it isn’t saved in Fikko, and we don’t send your name or profile with it), only the foods you choose to add. Recipe ideas send the ingredients you type in, the recipe types you pick, and your diet and allergies, so the ideas are safe for you to eat; nothing else from your profile or logs, and not your name. Only the ideas you choose to keep are saved, privately, in Fikko. The medication interaction check sends only the names on your medications and supplements list, and only when something on it isn’t on Fikko’s built-in list. Anthropic doesn’t use this data to train its models.
  • Your browser’s speech recognition for voice check-ins. When you tap the microphone, your browser turns your speech into text, which may involve your browser’s maker (such as Google for Chrome or Apple for Safari) processing the audio under their own privacy terms. Fikko never receives or stores the audio itself, only the text.
  • Vercel to host the app.
  • Google if you choose to sign in with Google, and to load the app’s font.
  • USDA FoodData Central for food search. Only your search words are sent, from our server, never your identity.
  • Wearable and health-app providers, only for devices you choose to connect once sync is available (for example Apple Health or Garmin). Their own privacy policies cover the data they hold.

Transfers outside your country

Fikko’s providers store and process data outside Korea, and possibly outside the country you live in. We use them under contracts that require them to protect your data and use it only to provide their service to us.

  • Supabase, Inc. (a US company; our database is stored on its servers in Singapore): your account, profile, logs, posts and photos, stored for as long as you have an account, to run the app.
  • Anthropic, PBC (United States, privacy@anthropic.com): what’s described above for the AI coach, voice check-ins, photo logging, recipe ideas and the medication check, sent over an encrypted connection each time you use those features, to produce and check a reply. Anthropic doesn’t use it to train its models and keeps it only for a limited period under its commercial terms, then deletes it. You can refuse this transfer by not using the AI features; the rest of Fikko works without them.
  • Vercel, Inc. (United States): requests passing through our servers and standard server logs, to host the app.
  • Google LLC (United States): your email address if you sign in with Google, and your device data if you connect Fitbit or Pixel Watch.

Data is sent over encrypted connections when you use the app or the feature. You can avoid the optional transfers by not using the AI features, Google sign-in or connected devices. The rest are needed to run Fikko, so the only way to stop them is to delete your account.

Data from Google

Fikko’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google health data only to show you your own trends in Fikko. We don’t sell it, use it for advertising, or let people read it, except with your permission, for security, or where the law requires.

If Fikko is sold or merged

If Fikko or the PipePiper business is sold, becomes part of another company (for example, if PipePiper becomes a corporation), or its assets are sold, your data may be transferred as part of that change. If that happens:

  • We’ll tell you in the app and by email before your data is transferred, including who the new owner is and how to contact them.
  • You’ll have time to download your data or delete your account before the transfer, and you can refuse to have your data transferred.
  • The new owner must keep the promises in this policy for the data we collected under it, including never selling your data or using it for advertising.
  • If they want to use your data in a new way, they’ll need your consent first.

During the deal, we only share what’s needed to evaluate it, under a confidentiality agreement.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, it’s deactivated straight away and permanently deleted after 30 days. Signing back in during those 30 days lets you restore it. After that, your profile, logs, posts, recipes, photos and points are deleted. Copies in our providers’ routine backups are removed as those backups expire. If you tell us why you’re leaving, we keep that reason without anything that identifies you.

Your rights and choices

  • Access and copy: download all your data from Profile → Account → Export my data.
  • Correct: edit your details and preferences in Profile at any time.
  • Delete: delete your account from Profile → Account. After the 30-day grace period, this can’t be undone.
  • Disconnect devices: disconnect any device from Profile → Connected devices at any time. This revokes our access, and you can choose to delete the data already synced.
  • Withdraw consent or ask us to stop: you can withdraw your consent, or ask us to stop processing your data, at any time by deleting your account or emailing us.

For anything you can’t do in the app, email hello@fikko.io. We’ll respond within the time your local law allows, and never later than 30 days. Someone you authorise, such as a parent or legal representative, can make a request for you.

If you’re not happy with our answer, you can complain to your privacy regulator:

  • Korea: the Personal Information Protection Commission’s Privacy Call Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, phone 118) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (kopico.go.kr).
  • Australia: the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
  • Singapore: the Personal Data Protection Commission (pdpc.gov.sg).
  • United States: if we turn down your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer. If you’re still not satisfied, you can contact your state attorney general.

Consumer health data (United States)

This section is our consumer health data privacy policy under Washington’s My Health My Data Act and similar laws in Nevada and Connecticut.

  • What we collect: body measurements and weight, meals, activity, sleep, mood, medications and supplements you list, and data from devices you connect, such as heart rate, HRV, sleep stages and blood oxygen.
  • Where it comes from: you, and the wearable services you choose to connect.
  • Why: to show you your own habits and insights and, if you turn them on, to run the AI features.
  • Who we share it with: only the service providers listed above, which process it on our behalf. We don’t sell it or share it with anyone else.
  • Your rights: see what we hold and who it’s been shared with, withdraw consent, and have it deleted, using the options under “Your rights and choices”.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and every table is protected so members can only read and change what they’re allowed to. Access keys for connected devices are stored where only our server can read them, never in your browser. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Fikko is for people aged 14 and over. We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 14. If you believe a child under 14 has created an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Not medical advice

Fikko is a general wellness app. Calorie targets, insights and the AI coach’s replies help you build everyday habits; they are not medical advice, and the AI coach is an AI, not a health professional. Talk to a qualified professional before making major changes to your diet, exercise or medication.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we’ll update the date above and let you know in the app.
