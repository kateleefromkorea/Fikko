Refund Policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

This policy covers Fikko Premium and Max subscriptions. Fikko is run by PipePiper, a sole proprietorship registered in the Republic of Korea (business registration number 471-45-01158; 104-106, 127 Irwon-ro, Gangnam-gu, Seoul 06361). Our online reseller and Merchant of Record, Paddle.com, processes your payment. It doesn’t change anything below.

The short version

  • 14-day money-back guarantee: ask within 14 days of any payment and we’ll refund that payment in full, whether it’s your first payment or a renewal.
  • After 14 days: you only pay for the days you use. We refund the unused days, counted by the day.
  • Cancel any time in Profile → Manage billing. Your plan then runs to the end of the period you’ve paid for, and nothing more is charged.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email hello@fikko.io from the address on your Fikko account, and say you’d like a refund. You don’t need to give a reason.
  2. We’ll end your plan and issue the refund through Paddle, usually within 2 business days. We’ll email you to confirm.
  3. The refund goes back to the card or payment method you used. Your bank or card provider may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

You can also contact Paddle, our payment provider, at paddle.net for billing questions or to find your invoice. Refund requests sent to either of us are handled under this policy.

What you get back

  • Within 14 days of a payment: the full amount you paid, including any sales tax, VAT or GST on it.
  • After 14 days: the part of the period you haven’t used, counted by the day from the day you ask. For example, if you ask 90 days into a yearly plan, you pay for 90 days and the rest is refunded, with the matching share of tax.
  • Charged by mistake: if you were charged twice, charged after you cancelled, or charged an amount you didn’t expect, tell us and we’ll refund it in full.

Cancelling

  • Cancel in Profile → Manage billing, or email us and we’ll cancel it for you.
  • Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep your plan until the end of the period you’ve paid for, unless you ask us to end it straight away and refund the unused days.
  • When your plan ends, your account moves to the Free plan. Your data stays.
  • If you delete your account, your subscription is cancelled automatically so it can’t renew.

Things that aren’t charged, so there’s nothing to refund

  • The Free plan costs nothing, and so does the 7-day Premium trial. We never charge you unless you choose a paid plan and give your payment details.
  • Founding members get Premium free for 12 months. Nothing is charged during that year.

Price changes

If we change a price, we’ll tell you at least 30 days before it applies to you, so you can cancel first.

Your legal rights

This policy doesn’t limit any rights you have under the consumer law where you live, including any right to cancel or to a remedy for a faulty service.
